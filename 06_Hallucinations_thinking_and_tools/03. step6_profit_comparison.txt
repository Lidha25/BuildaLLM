Step 6 — Retailer profit comparison
Model: GPT-6.2 Sol

Question
A retailer buys 1,247 units at €38.75 each. It sells 83% of them, rounded down to whole units, at €64.90 each with a 12% discount. Customers return 7% of the sold units, rounded down, for full refunds; those units cannot be resold. Unsold units sell for €21.40 each. Shipping costs €2.85 per unit originally bought, and fixed costs are €6,730. What is the final profit? Round only the final amount to two decimals.

Light — Give only the final answer
€930.46

Light — Show the calculation before answering
1. Units initially sold = ⌊1,247 × 83%⌋ = 1,035.
2. Units returned = ⌊1,035 × 7%⌋ = 72, leaving 963 sales after refunds.
3. Net revenue from discounted sales = 963 × €64.90 × 88% = €54,998.856.
4. Revenue from unsold units = (1,247 − 1,035) × €21.40 = €4,536.80.
5. Total costs = 1,247 × (€38.75 + €2.85) + €6,730 = €58,605.20.
Final profit = €54,998.856 + €4,536.80 − €58,605.20 = €930.456, which rounds to €930.46.

4. Profit before rounding. €59,535.656 − €58,605.20 = €930.456.
Final profit: €930.46.
